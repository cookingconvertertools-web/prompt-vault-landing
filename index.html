Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Prompt Vault: AI Manager is a Chrome Extension designed to help you manage and inject AI prompts. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Email: Collected only if you choose to sign in to enable Team Sharing or Cloud Backup features.
  • Prompt Data: Stored locally in your browser's `chrome.storage` by default.
    • If you enable Team Sharing, prompts are securely stored in our Supabase database, strictly isolated by your unique Team ID so only your team members can access them.
    • If you enable Google Drive Backup, a copy of your library is saved directly to your personal Google Drive, giving you full ownership and portability of your data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email solely for authentication and account management. We do not sell, share, or analyze your prompt data for advertising or training purposes.

3. Data Security

All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS. We utilize Supabase's secure authentication and Google's official OAuth APIs. Your Google Drive backup is stored in your personal Drive, not our servers.
